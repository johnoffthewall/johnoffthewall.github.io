John Off the Wall

When I'm off the clock, I spend my time cooking, watching comedy films, or catching up with childhood friends. I lean introverted, but I bring my own low-key sense of humor and good energy once we connect. My approach to everything comes down to curiosity and learning directly by doing.

I want to live a full life spent charting unfamiliar roads, exploring new places, and eventually building a home by the ocean or deep in nature. Learning about different cultures through the personal stories of locals constantly fascinates me. This platform exists to document that journey and uncover who I am outside of my work.

I have come to realize I am no longer in a season focused on speed. It is about overcoming obstacles now. Instead of racing to a finish line, I focus on navigating the difficult climbs, much like the steep, winding pass of Mt. Kilang, the Blazing Mountain of the Gods. Right now, my priority is building endurance, staying curious, and enjoying the simple comfort of home-cooked meals, solid laughs, and lifelong friends.

Tech Obsessive

I started tweaking icons on my Nokia 6680 as a kid, just messing around with wallpapers and themes. When I got my first Android phone, the Galaxy Y, I immediately flashed Hyperion ROM because I wanted something cleaner. Been doing it ever since. CyanogenMod, LineageOS, PixelExperience, EvolutionX, Derpfest, CrDroid, PixelOS. I just prefer stock Android.

I read tech articles about phones, PCs, keyboards, and reviews. Watch YouTube videos about it too. Not because I'm buying everything, just because I'm interested. I like understanding how things work and what's new in tech.

My Nothing Phone 2 is good enough that I don't need to flash a ROM anymore. But I still tinker with my older phones running CrDroid and Derpfest. I like the whole process of flashing, testing, seeing what works. Respect the dev community for keeping old devices alive.

It's just something I enjoy doing. When I see something interesting in tech, I dive into it. Learn how it works, test it out, figure things out. That's pretty much it.

Self-Taught Creative

I'm not a professional photographer or editor. I just see something cool and think, "I want to know how to do that."

Color grading in Lightroom? I watched YouTube videos and practiced. Photo manipulation in Photoshop? Same thing. Video color grading and transitions? Figured it out. Made custom t-shirts for my friends' anniversaries. The process is always the same: I get curious, I look it up, I try it, and eventually I get it done.

I can teach myself stuff pretty fast. I don't need to be an expert before I start, I just start and learn along the way. When I see something I like, I break it down and figure out how it works. Try the tool, practice, apply it, repeat.

It's not complicated. I'm just interested in how things work, so I learn them. That's it.

Two Wheels & Open Roads

I like traveling, motorcycles, cars, cycling, basketball, exploring. These are things I enjoy, so they don't feel like work.

I'm always picking up stories and experiences from traveling and riding. Every ride has something different about it, every road trip is its own thing. Exploring new places, feeling the freedom on the road, the rush of riding, it all shapes how I see things.

Motorcycles and cars give me that sense of freedom. Cycling and basketball keep me moving and sharp. Traveling ties it all together. I'm not just going places, I'm collecting experiences and memories.

It's just a part of who I am. Need to be on the move, exploring, doing stuff.

Grind & Chill

I play competitive games like Counter Strike, Dota 2, Half-Life, NBA 2K, and other FPS games. I like strategy, the mechanics, the competition. Recently got into poker too because the strategy and mind games are interesting.

On the flip side, I rewatch shows like The Office, The Big Bang Theory, How I Met Your Mother, Community, Modern Family, and Brooklyn Nine-Nine. I watch anime. It's just something I do to chill and unwind. Not about catching everything new, just comfort watching.

It's a balance. I need the competitive stuff to stay sharp, and I need the comfort shows to decompress. Both matter.
